6. Reusable authenticated fixture design

authenticated_route_session configures temporary production SQLite authentication
and Investigation repositories through normal dependency injection. It creates a
unique researcher through POST /api/v1/auth/accounts, retains the issued cookies,
persists requested parent Investigations for the returned account ID, exposes the
real CSRF cookie as X-ISEES-CSRF, and rewrites descriptive STUDIO principalId fields
to the authenticated account for ordinary fixture requests. Explicit mismatch tests
use the raw client so body identity remains non-authoritative. Overrides are restored
key-by-key, preserving unrelated suite overrides and preventing global leakage.

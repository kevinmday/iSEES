\# SYSTEM CANON



\# SC-006 — Research Authoring Framework



Version: Canon v1



Status: RATIFIED



\---



\# PURPOSE



The Research workspace is the production authoring environment of iSEES.



Its purpose is to transform deterministic investigations into professional research products.



Research is where investigators create reports, white papers, technical analyses, executive briefings, working notes, and publications derived from the canonical investigation.



\---



\# DESIGN PRINCIPLE



The product of an investigation is the investigator's work.



The canonical investigation represents reality as understood by iSEES.



The Research Document represents the investigator's interpretation, analysis, and conclusions.



These are intentionally different objects.



\---



\# PRIMARY OBJECT



The primary object of the Research workspace is:



ResearchDocument



The document is the center of the workspace.



All other tools exist to support its creation.



\---



\# WORKSPACE



The Research workspace is an authoring environment.



Typical capabilities include:



\- document editing

\- note taking

\- evidence citation

\- references

\- document organization

\- export

\- publication



The Operator Assistant assists the author but never replaces them.



\---



\# OPERATOR ASSISTANT



The Operator Assistant is advisory only.



It may:



\- summarize evidence

\- rewrite text

\- suggest citations

\- compare investigations

\- improve readability

\- assist research



It never modifies the canonical investigation.



All suggested changes remain explicit and reviewable.



\---



\# VERSIONING



Research Documents use a Git-like revision model.



Documents support:



\- commit history

\- undo/restore

\- revision comparison

\- branching

\- merge

\- provenance



Every published document can be traced back to the exact investigation revision from which it was produced.



\---



\# EXPORT



Research Documents are publishable products.



Supported export targets may include:



\- PDF

\- DOCX

\- Markdown

\- HTML

\- Presentation



Future formats may be added without changing the underlying document model.



\---



\# CANON



The Research workspace is not an AI chat.



It is the professional authoring environment of iSEES.



The document is the product.



The investigation is the source of truth.



The assistant is an editorial tool.

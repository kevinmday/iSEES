I think this deserves a \*\*new System Canon\*\*, not just an addition to SC-007. SC-007 established the \*\*Investigation Workbench and AI-assist philosophy\*\*. What we've been discussing is the architecture \*\*upstream of the corpus\*\*—how an investigation expands into the unknown while remaining completely deterministic.



I would make this the next canonical document:



> \*\*SC-008 — Deterministic Discovery and Investigative Expansion Framework\*\*



I don't think this should be written as a feature specification. It should establish the \*\*laws\*\* that every future Discovery Runtime, Federation Runtime, and Repository Adapter must obey.



For example, I'd organize it like this:



\---



\# SC-008 — Deterministic Discovery and Investigative Expansion Framework



\## Purpose



Define the canonical architecture governing how iSEES discovers new information while preserving deterministic, inspectable investigation.



\---



\## Principle 1 — The Manifold Is the Source of Discovery



Traditional search begins with keywords.



iSEES begins with the current state of the Investigation Manifold.



The evolving topology of the investigation determines where discovery occurs next.



\---



\## Principle 2 — Discovery Is Deterministic



Discovery is computation.



Discovery is not AI reasoning.



Given identical:



\* investigation state

\* corpus

\* repositories

\* search configuration



the Discovery Runtime shall produce identical discovery plans.



\---



\## Principle 3 — AI Never Participates in Investigation



This is worth stating unambiguously.



AI shall never



\* create evidence

\* infer relationships

\* resolve entities

\* rank investigative truth

\* establish hypotheses

\* modify topology

\* compute investigative metrics



AI may only consume deterministic outputs to produce human-readable artifacts such as reports, summaries, or explanations.



\---



\## Principle 4 — Investigation Drives Search



Search is not independent.



Search is a projection of investigative state.



The Discovery Runtime computes search vectors from the topology of the Investigation Manifold.



\---



\## Principle 5 — Discovery Produces Candidates



Discovery never modifies the corpus.



Its only product is



\*\*CandidateArtifact\*\*



Candidate artifacts require operator review before entering the corpus.



\---



\## Principle 6 — Repository Federation Is a Knowledge Graph



Repositories are first-class objects.



Every repository possesses deterministic metadata describing:



\* subject domains

\* geography

\* chronology

\* language

\* authority

\* media types

\* interfaces

\* historical yield



Repositories therefore become navigable topology.



\---



\## Principle 7 — Discovery Expands Frontiers



The purpose of discovery is not finding answers.



The purpose of discovery is expanding the boundary between known and unknown.



The Discovery Runtime continuously identifies investigative frontiers.



\---



\## Principle 8 — Mathematical Foundations



SC-008 should explicitly say:



iSEES adopts existing mathematics whenever applicable.



Including:



\* graph theory

\* information retrieval

\* knowledge graph traversal

\* deterministic ranking

\* ontology mapping

\* similarity metrics

\* indexing



Innovation occurs only where existing mathematics does not describe investigative expansion.



\---



\## Principle 9 — Investigation Mathematics



This is the part I think is genuinely new.



Rather than inventing another AI, iSEES defines deterministic quantities describing the state of an investigation.



Examples include:



\* Evidence Coverage

\* Frontier Width

\* Discovery Potential

\* Repository Affinity

\* Artifact Yield

\* Provenance Density

\* Topological Expansion Rate

\* Investigative Entropy

\* Knowledge Gradient



These are not conclusions.



They are measurable properties of the investigation itself.



\---



\## Principle 10 — Complete Inspectability



Every discovery recommendation must be explainable.



Example:



```text

Repository:

XYZ Monastery Archive



Reason:



Religion Cluster ............ 0.92



Time Period Match ........... 0.87



Language Match .............. 1.00



Geographic Match ............ 0.73



Historical Yield ............ 0.81



Final Score ................. 4.33

```



Nothing hidden.



Nothing probabilistic.



Nothing learned.

\# The biggest realization



I think we've actually arrived at something larger than "Discovery."



SC-001 through SC-007 established \*\*how iSEES reasons over what it already knows\*\*.



SC-008 would establish \*\*how iSEES deterministically discovers what it does not yet know\*\*.



That completes the architecture.



```text

Repositories



↓



Discovery Runtime



↓



Candidate Artifacts



↓



Corpus



↓



Resolve–Dissolve Computation



↓



Investigation Manifold



↓



Selection Intelligence



↓



Research Desk



↓



Authoring Studio



↓



AI Report Generation

```



Notice what is \*not\* in that pipeline.



There is \*\*no AI cognition layer\*\*.



There is \*\*no probabilistic investigation layer\*\*.



There is \*\*no opaque reasoning engine\*\*.



The investigation is computational from beginning to end.



AI appears only at the very end, where it transforms deterministic investigative state into human communication.



\---



One final thought: I would be careful with the title "Discovery Engine." That describes a software component. What we've been discussing is more fundamental. This canon defines an \*\*epistemology\*\*—a theory of how an investigation expands its knowledge while preserving provenance, reproducibility, and operator authority. That's why I think it belongs in the System Canon alongside SC-001 through SC-007, rather than as an implementation document.

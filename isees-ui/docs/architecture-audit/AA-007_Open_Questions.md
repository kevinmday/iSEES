\# Mission



This document records unresolved architectural questions identified during reconstruction of the iSEES platform.



Unlike the System Canon, which defines accepted architectural truth, or the Architectural Audit, which reconstructs the current architecture, this document captures questions whose answers remain under investigation.



Its purpose is to preserve uncertainty rather than prematurely resolve it.



Architectural uncertainty is considered a valuable engineering artifact.



\---



\# Purpose



As the platform evolves, new architectural questions naturally emerge.



Some questions require:



\- additional engineering

\- additional experimentation

\- implementation experience

\- literature review

\- operational validation



Rather than forcing immediate answers, these questions should be preserved until sufficient evidence exists.



This document becomes the architectural research backlog for future engineering work.



\---



\# Architectural Philosophy



Questions are not architectural weaknesses.



They represent opportunities for improved understanding.



No architectural question should be closed simply to complete an engineering package.



Questions should remain open until supported by:



\- implementation

\- experimentation

\- architectural consensus

\- computational validation

\- documented engineering rationale

\# Decision Process



Architectural questions should be resolved through one or more of the following:



\- System Canon revision

\- Engineering implementation

\- Prototype validation

\- Innovation Review

\- Architectural Audit

\- Formal computational analysis



Architectural decisions should never be based solely upon implementation convenience.



\---



\# Relationship to Other Documentation



Questions originating during implementation should be recorded here before architectural modification.



When resolved:



\- accepted architectural decisions migrate into the System Canon;

\- architectural rationale is preserved within the Innovation Review;

\- the resulting architecture is reflected in the Architectural Audit; and

\- implementation proceeds through a future Engineering Package.



This process preserves a complete and traceable architectural history from question to implementation.



\---



\# Living Research Backlog



This document intentionally remains incomplete.



As iSEES evolves, new architectural questions will emerge.



Those questions should be recorded immediately rather than resolved prematurely.



Accordingly, this document represents the ongoing architectural research agenda of the iSEES platform.



Its purpose is not to eliminate uncertainty.



Its purpose is to ensure uncertainty is managed deliberately, investigated rigorously, and resolved through evidence rather than assumption.

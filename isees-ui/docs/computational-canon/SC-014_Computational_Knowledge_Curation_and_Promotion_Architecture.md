\# 1. ABSTRACT



This document establishes the canonical Computational Knowledge

Curation and Promotion Architecture for iSEES.



The architecture introduces a new computational phase between

Research and Authoring.



Research is intentionally temporary.



Knowledge is intentionally permanent.



The transition between the two is not automatic.



Instead, the operator intentionally promotes collected research

artifacts into durable Computational Knowledge Objects (KOs).



This distinction preserves investigative flexibility while

maintaining a rigorously curated computational knowledge base.



Knowledge Mode is therefore not another visualization of the

investigation graph.



Knowledge Mode is the computational foundry where research is

transformed into canonical knowledge.



\---



\# 2. MOTIVATION



Without an explicit curation phase, investigative systems often

collapse into one of two undesirable outcomes.



Either every observation immediately becomes "knowledge," producing

an ever-growing repository of unverified information,



or investigators continuously work from temporary notes that never

evolve into reusable computational assets.



Neither supports long-term computational reasoning.



iSEES intentionally separates these concerns.



Research captures possibility.



Knowledge captures intentional certainty.



\---

\---



\# 4. RESEARCH



Research is a temporary computational workspace.



Research answers the question



"What have I collected?"



Research objects are candidates.



Research objects are expected to be



• added



• removed



• reorganized



• ignored



• discarded



without affecting the canonical knowledge corpus.



The Research Inbox therefore functions as a computational staging

area rather than a permanent repository.



\---



\# 5. KNOWLEDGE



Knowledge represents intentionally curated computational objects.



Knowledge answers the question



"What do I know?"



Knowledge Objects are expected to possess



• identity



• provenance



• confidence



• relationships



• revision history



• lifecycle



• computational ownership



Knowledge therefore represents persistent computational memory.



\---



\# 6. PROMOTION



Promotion is the intentional transition from Research into

Knowledge.



Promotion is never automatic.



Promotion always requires explicit operator intent.



The operator determines



• whether promotion occurs



• what computational type is created



• associated metadata



• confidence



• tags



• relationships



Promotion therefore represents computational authorship rather

than passive storage.



\---



\# 7. KNOWLEDGE OBJECT CREATION



Promotion creates a new Knowledge Object.



The Knowledge Object is not merely a copy of an investigative node.



Instead it becomes an independent computational entity.



Example



Research



USS Nimitz



↓



Promote



↓



Knowledge Object



Entity



USS Nimitz



The resulting Knowledge Object acquires its own computational

identity independent of the originating graph node while preserving

complete provenance.



\---



\# 8. COMPOSITE KNOWLEDGE OBJECTS



A Knowledge Object may also be composed from multiple Research

Example

The resulting Knowledge Object represents a higher-order

Composite Knowledge Objects become first-class computational

entities throughout the platform.

\# 10. KNOWLEDGE MODE



Knowledge Mode is not another graph browser.



Knowledge Mode is not another inspector.



Knowledge Mode is not another metadata viewer.



Instead, Knowledge Mode exists for



• promotion



• curation



• composition



• revision



• lifecycle management



• computational knowledge engineering



Knowledge therefore occupies a unique role within iSEES.



\---



\# 11. PERSISTENT OPERATOR INSTRUMENTS



Knowledge Mode does not duplicate existing operator instruments.



Persistent instruments remain available across all workspace modes.



Left Instrument



Explorer



Right Instrument



Inspector



Only the center workspace changes.



When entering Knowledge Mode



Explorer displays Knowledge Objects.



Inspector displays the selected computational element.



The center workspace provides computational knowledge curation.



This preserves operator muscle memory while preventing interface

duplication.



\---

Knowledge Workspace therefore becomes the canonical environment for

\---



\# 13. AUTHOR INTEGRATION



Author Studio consumes Knowledge Objects rather than temporary

Research artifacts.



Authors therefore reference



Knowledge Objects



rather than



raw investigative nodes.



This provides stable computational references throughout the

authoring lifecycle.



\---



\# 14. AI+ INTEGRATION



AI+ operates against curated Computational Knowledge Objects rather

than the temporary Research Inbox.



This significantly improves reasoning quality by constraining

analysis to intentionally promoted computational knowledge.



Research remains exploratory.



Knowledge becomes the computational reasoning substrate.

\# 16. CANONICAL PRINCIPLES



The following principles govern Computational Knowledge Curation.



1\. Research is temporary.



2\. Knowledge is intentional.



3\. Promotion is explicit.



4\. Knowledge Objects are independent computational entities.



5\. Composite Knowledge Objects are first-class computational

artifacts.



6\. Persistent operator instruments remain unchanged across

workspace modes.



7\. Knowledge Mode performs computational knowledge engineering,

not graph exploration.



8\. Author Studio consumes Knowledge Objects rather than temporary

Research artifacts.



9\. AI+ reasons over curated computational knowledge rather than

unfiltered investigative collections.



10\. Computational knowledge represents the permanent memory of the

iSEES platform.

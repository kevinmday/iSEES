P24 MANIFOLD NAVIGATION DOCTRINE



Status: Architectural Direction (Locked)



The manifold is not a visualization layer.



The manifold is the primary investigation navigation surface for iSEES.



Nodes represent knowledge entities including events, artifacts, facilities, sensors, organizations, witnesses, hypotheses, locations, and temporal markers.



Edges represent explainable deterministic relationships and are first-class investigation entities.



Clicking a node should reveal meaning, context, supporting evidence, relationships, and investigation pathways.



Clicking an edge should reveal why two entities are connected, including similarity contributions, supporting evidence, contradictions, and manifold dimensions.



Folding and unfolding are not visual effects. They represent changes in investigative perspective and coordinate systems.



Examples include:



\* Narrative View

\* Observability View

\* Infrastructure View

\* Temporal View

\* Geographic View

\* Hypothesis View

\* Artifact View



The workspace pivots around manifold interaction.



The manifold drives the workspace.



The workspace does not drive the manifold.



All manifold structures must be derived from deterministic inspectable mathematics.

The manifold serves as the bridge between mathematical relationships and human understanding.



Future UI direction should move toward a graph-native investigation environment where relationships become navigable knowledge rather than static visualizations.
